Young Girls Lead

Young Girls Lead provides inclusive experiences that help girls ages 10-16 discover their strengths, cultivate curiosity, and lead with confidence.

What We Offer

STEM Exploration

Hands-on workshops and mentorship that ignite interest in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

Creative Studios

Arts, storytelling, and design experiences that build communication and collaboration skills.

Wellness Circles

Safe circles where girls explore social-emotional learning, self-esteem, and mindfulness.

Get Involved

We partner with schools, guardians, and volunteers to offer weekend and holiday programs. Reach out to bring Young Girls Lead to your community.
