Nurturing Purpose, Courage, and Compassion in Every Heart

Teresa Heart Foundation is a non-profit empowering young women and girls through leadership, education, and purpose-driven programs across Africa. We believe in creating safe spaces where voices are heard, potential is nourished, and dreams are realized.
